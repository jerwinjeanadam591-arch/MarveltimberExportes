About Pinewood

Cost-effective softwood suited to structural and non-structural applications. Our pinewood is carefully sourced, kiln-dried and graded to meet international standards for export markets.

Pine timber offers excellent value for construction, packaging and industrial uses where performance and economy are both important factors.

Key characteristics

  • Color: Pale to light brown
  • Grain: Relatively uniform and straight
  • Density: 510 kg/m³ — lightweight yet strong
  • Workability: Excellent — easy to machine and finish

Product specifications

Dimensions

Flexible sizing to accommodate diverse industrial needs and end-market requirements.

  • Thickness: 18–50 mm
  • Width: 75–300 mm
  • Length: 2–6 metres

Grades

Standard export grades optimised for structural and general purpose applications.

  • Industrial Grade
  • Select Grade (appearance)
  • Construction Grade (strength)

Moisture & drying

Kiln-dried to stable levels for immediate shipment and end-use.

  • Moisture content: 12–18%
  • Fast drying schedule available
  • Certification included

Applications & industries

Construction & framing

Structural timber for roofing, flooring and general building framing across residential and commercial projects.

Panel & board production

Core material for plywood, MDF and particle board manufacturing with consistent grain and dimensional stability.

Packaging & pallets

Durable pallet boards and shipping crate components for industrial logistics and export operations.
