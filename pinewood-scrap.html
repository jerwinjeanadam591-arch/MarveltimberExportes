About Pinewood Scrap

Economical scrap timber sourced from our processing lines — residual pieces, trim waste and off-cuts that are sized and sorted for reuse in composite board production, biomass energy and pulping applications.

Our scrap is carefully graded to remove foreign material and delivered in consistent bulk lots, making it ideal for buyers seeking reliable feedstock for downstream manufacturing.

Key characteristics

  • Source: Processing mill waste streams
  • Sorting: Graded by size fraction
  • Quality: Contaminant-free, inspected
  • Volume: Bulk supply in consistent lots

Product specifications

Size fractions

Sorted into consistent size categories for specific end-use applications.

  • Chip material: 25–75 mm
  • Mixed fractions available
  • Custom sizing on request

Quality grading

Inspected and sorted to remove metal, stone and other contaminants.

  • Foreign material: <0.5%
  • Moisture: variable (as-received)
  • Density sorted options

Moisture content

Supplied in variable moisture conditions suitable for multiple end-uses.

  • Green (wet) material available
  • Air-dried fractions
  • Custom drying on request
