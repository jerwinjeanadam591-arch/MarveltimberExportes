Our Services

Services supporting export supply

Integrated processing, finishing and logistics services designed for reliable, quality-driven export supply.

Saw Milling

High-capacity saw lines with precision equipment enabling consistent dimensional quality and optimal yield from raw log material. Our milling teams optimize stock sizes to meet buyer specifications and minimize waste.

  • • Precision blade technology for clean cuts
  • • Skilled operators with quality training
  • • Tailored dimension profiles
  • • Waste recovery programmes

Kiln Drying

Climate-controlled drying facilities ensure precise moisture content reduction and minimize checking/warping. Our drying programme reduces rejection rates on arrival and improves dimensional stability for end-users.

  • • Modern kiln technology with monitoring
  • • Custom drying schedules available
  • • Moisture certificates provided
  • • Rapid turnaround schedules

Planing & Finishing

Value-added finishing services transform sawn timber into factory-ready components. Planing, moulding and edge-profiling reduce buyer costs and accelerate their manufacturing schedules.

  • • Four-square planing to specification
  • • Custom moulding profiles
  • • Edge sanding and profiling
  • • Ready-to-use component delivery

Bulk Supply

Large-lot supply with professional packing, marking and documentation to meet international importer standards. We coordinate storage, staging and on-time shipment release.

  • • Large-scale order consolidation
  • • Custom packing per buyer spec
  • • Marked bundles and labels
  • • Staged delivery coordination

Export Logistics

Experienced freight coordination from factory to port and beyond. We manage booking, documentation, customs clearance and door-to-door delivery with proven freight partners.

  • • Freight booking and rate negotiation
  • • Export documentation & compliance
  • • Customs clearance support
  • • Door-to-port and port-to-door options

Integrated supply solutions

From raw material to on-time delivery, our end-to-end services streamline your supply chain and reduce buyer friction. Talk to our team about customizing a supply programme for your market.
