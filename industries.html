Industries we serve

We partner with manufacturers and traders across diverse sectors, delivering tailored timber and agro solutions.

Construction & Joinery

We supply structural timber and premium joinery materials for contractors, builders and specialized joiners working on residential, commercial and institutional projects.

From rough-sawn framing timber to finished door and window components, our supply programmes ensure consistency and on-time delivery to project sites.

  • • Structural beams and posts
  • • Framing and flooring joists
  • • Premium door and window frames
  • • Custom joinery components

Furniture & Interior

Premium hardwood suppliers for furniture manufacturers, interior designers and fitout specialists creating high-end residential and commercial environments.

We provide consistent graded hardwoods and specialty veneers with careful attention to aesthetic requirements and dimensional precision for complex fabrication.

  • • Solid hardwood selections (teak, etc.)
  • • Veneer and panel materials
  • • Bespoke moulding and trim
  • • Colour and grain-matched sets

Agro Processing

We supply food-grade agricultural commodities — grains, seeds, spices and pulses — with full traceability, phytosanitary certification and quality assurance for food and feed processors.

Our supply chains are audited for food safety and our documentation meets international import requirements for sensitive markets.

  • • Certified organic where available
  • • Bulk grain and flour mill supply
  • • Spice and seed processors
  • • Full audit trail documentation

Retail & Distribution

Flexible supply programmes for wholesalers and retail distributors seeking diversified product ranges and manageable lot sizes for end-customers.

  • • Branded packaging options
