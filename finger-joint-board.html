Finger Joint Board

Dimensionally stable, longer-length timber components for moulding and joinery applications.

About Finger Joint Board

Finger-jointed boards are created by joining shorter timber lengths end-to-end using precision finger-jointing techniques, then laminating them to create stable, long-length components perfect for mouldings, skirtings and detailed joinery.

The finger joint construction minimises waste and improves dimensional stability compared to solid timber, making it economical and reliable for high-volume manufacturing.

Key characteristics

  • Construction: Finger-jointed lengths laminated to size
  • Lengths: Up to 7+ metres continuously
  • Stability: Superior dimensional consistency
  • Finish: Ready for paint or clear coat
