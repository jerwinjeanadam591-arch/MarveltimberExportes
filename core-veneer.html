Core Veneer

Consistent veneer sheets for interior design and panel lamination applications.

About Core Veneer

High-quality veneer material sourced from premium hardwood species and processed with precision slicing and grading techniques. Ideal for furniture makers and interior specialists requiring consistent finish.

Our veneer stock is dried and stored in climate-controlled conditions to maintain dimensional stability and aesthetic qualities throughout transit and use.

Key characteristics

  • Species: Mixed hardwood selections
  • Thickness: Precision-sliced 0.6–1.5 mm
  • Finish: Flat-sliced for grain consistency
  • Quality: Minimal defects and knots

Product specifications

Dimensions

Standard packing sizes for immediate use or custom cutting by fabricators.

  • Thickness: 0.6–1.5 mm
  • Width: 100–200 mm standard
  • Length: 2–4 metre coils

Grading & sorting

Selected for colour consistency and aesthetic appeal with minimal character marks.

  • Premium grade (A+)
  • First grade (A) standard
  • Species-matched bundles

Packaging

Carefully packed in protective layers to maintain flatness and prevent warping during shipment.

  • Coil or reel packs
  • Flat sheet stacking
  • Climate-sealed cartons

Applications & industries

Furniture & cabinetry

Veneer facing for cabinet doors, drawer fronts and premium furniture with consistent grain matching.

Interior design

Architectural panels, wall cladding and decorative applications for high-end residential and commercial interiors.

Panel lamination

Core material for plywood and engineered panel production with controlled appearance.
