Pinewood Block Board

About Pinewood Block Board

Engineered panel composed of solid wood strips (blocks) glued together and faced with veneer or plywood. Offers superior strength-to-weight ratio and dimensional stability for applications requiring large flat surfaces.

Our block board is manufactured to strict tolerances with high-quality adhesives and facing materials, making it ideal for furniture, fitouts and commercial construction.

Key characteristics

  • Core: Solid pine wood strips
  • Facing: Quality veneer or plywood laminate
  • Stability: Excellent flatness and strength
  • Workability: Can be machined, veneered or painted
