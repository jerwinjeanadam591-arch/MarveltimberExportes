Teakwood

High-end hardwood renowned for beauty, durability and exceptional weather resistance.

About Teakwood

Selected kiln-dried teak, graded to export standards. Our teakwood is sourced from managed forests and processed to ensure dimensional stability and superior finish quality.

Teak is prized for its natural oils, which make it highly resistant to decay, insects and weathering — ideal for both interior and exterior applications requiring long-term durability.

Key characteristics

  • Color: Golden to dark brown
  • Grain: Straight to interlocked
  • Density: 630 kg/m³ — stable and strong
  • Durability: Class 1 (extremely durable)

Product specifications

Dimensions

Available in sawn lengths, planks and beams. Custom sizes on request.

  • Thickness: 25–100 mm
  • Width: 75–300 mm
  • Length: 1–6 metres

Grading

Sorted to export standards ensuring consistent appearance and structural integrity.

  • Select Grade (best appearance)
  • Number 1 Grade (general use)
  • No knots or minimal character

Moisture & drying

Kiln-dried to controlled levels for immediate use and dimensional stability.

  • Moisture content: 10–14%
  • Custom drying available
  • Certified by dryer logs

Applications & industries

Doors & Joinery

Premium exterior and interior doors, window frames and detailed joinery work for high-end residential and commercial projects.

Furniture & Interiors

Fine furniture, cabinetry and architectural details for luxury interiors and bespoke commissioned pieces.

Outdoor structures

Decking, garden furniture and exterior cladding — teak's natural weather resistance makes it ideal for exposed applications.
